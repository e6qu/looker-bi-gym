# What does a DORA-shaped ICT third-party register row record for a banking BI dashboard?

## Front

What does a DORA-shaped ICT third-party register row record for a
banking BI dashboard?

## Back

The ICT service (e.g. BigQuery), the provider (e.g. Google Cloud), its
criticality classification (critical / important / supporting), the
function it supports, the data location, and an exit-plan summary. The
register is updated whenever any of those attributes change.
