# What two BI evidence items does the CRR CET1 ratio require for a same-period publication?

## Front

What two BI evidence items does the CRR CET1 ratio require for a
same-period publication?

## Back

CET1 capital and total risk-weighted exposure (RWA) for the same reporting
date, with reconciliation to the COREP capital adequacy template. Mixing
dates or RWA approaches between numerator and denominator produces a
ratio that cannot be compared period-over-period.
