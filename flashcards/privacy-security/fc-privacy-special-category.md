# Why does GDPR special-category data raise the bar even when the field is not needed for the metric?

## Front

Why does GDPR special-category data raise the bar even when the field is
not needed for the metric?

## Back

GDPR Article 9 sets stricter conditions for special-category personal data
(health, biometric, religious, etc.). Minimisation already removes it from
a dashboard source when it is not necessary; if a valid purpose is later
defined, the field re-enters under a separate access and security review,
not by relaxing the minimisation rule.
