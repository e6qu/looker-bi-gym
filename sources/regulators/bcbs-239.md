# BCBS 239 Source Cards

These cards support risk data aggregation and risk reporting context. They are
training material only, not legal, regulatory, accounting, privacy,
compliance, or model-risk advice.

## SRC-BCBS-239-PRINCIPLES

- Type: international supervisory standard.
- Publisher: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision.
- URL: https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs239.htm
- Accessed: 2026-05-16.
- Used by facts:
  - `FACT-BCBS-239-RDARR-PRINCIPLES`
- Relevant sections and quotes:
  - Section II - Overarching governance and infrastructure
    (Principles 1-2): the board and senior management approve the
    risk-data-aggregation framework; a bank's data architecture and
    IT infrastructure should fully support its risk-data-aggregation
    capabilities and risk-reporting practices.
  - Section III - Risk data aggregation capabilities
    (Principles 3-6): accuracy and integrity, completeness, timeliness,
    and adaptability of aggregated risk data.
  - Section IV - Risk reporting practices (Principles 7-11): accuracy,
    comprehensiveness, clarity and usefulness, frequency, and
    distribution of risk reports.
  - Section V - Supervisory review, tools and cooperation
    (Principles 12-14): supervisory review of compliance, remedial
    actions, and home / host cooperation.
- Notes: Use this source when banking BI feeds credit, market, or
  operational risk reporting. The 14-principle framework spans
  governance, aggregation capabilities, reporting practices, and
  supervisory review.
