## SRC-IFRS9-STANDARD

- Type: accounting standard.
- Publisher: IFRS Foundation.
- URL: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/
- Accessed: 2026-05-16.
- Used by facts:
  - `FACT-IFRS9-STAGES`
- Relevant sections and quotes:
  - Section 5.5 (impairment): an entity shall measure expected credit
    losses of a financial instrument in a way that reflects an
    unbiased and probability-weighted amount, the time value of money,
    and reasonable and supportable information available without undue
    cost or effort.
  - 5.5.3 / 5.5.5: at each reporting date the entity measures the loss
    allowance at an amount equal to the lifetime expected credit
    losses if the credit risk has increased significantly since
    initial recognition (stage 2); otherwise the loss allowance is
    measured at 12-month expected credit losses (stage 1).
  - 5.5.13 onward: stage 3 covers financial assets that are
    credit-impaired at the reporting date and uses lifetime ECL on the
    net carrying amount.
- Notes: Use this source for ECL staging and credit-risk BI reporting.
  The stage-1 / stage-2 / stage-3 split and the 12-month vs lifetime
  ECL distinction are supported by sections 5.5.3 - 5.5.13 of the
  standard.
