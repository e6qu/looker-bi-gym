# EU Capital Requirements Regulation Source Cards

These cards support EU prudential capital BI context. They are training material
only, not legal, regulatory, accounting, privacy, compliance, or model-risk
advice.

## SRC-CRR-ELI-2013-575

- Type: official EU law.
- Publisher: EUR-Lex.
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2013/575/oj
- Accessed: 2026-05-16.
- Used by facts:
  - `FACT-CRR-CET1-RATIO`
- Relevant articles and quotes:
  - Article 92(1)(a): institutions shall at all times satisfy a Common
    Equity Tier 1 capital ratio of 4,5 %.
  - Article 92(2)(a): the CET1 ratio is the Common Equity Tier 1 capital
    of the institution expressed as a percentage of the total risk
    exposure amount.
  - Article 92(3): definition of total risk exposure amount (sum of
    risk-weighted exposure amounts plus other components for
    operational, market, and CVA risk).
  - Article 26 onward: composition of Common Equity Tier 1 items.
- Notes: Use this source for capital-ratio BI reporting context. Pair
  with EBA reporting framework sources for COREP-template alignment.
  Note that buffer requirements (capital conservation, countercyclical,
  systemic) are added by the CRD package on top of the CRR Article 92
  minimum.

## SRC-PSD2-ELI-2015-2366

- Type: official EU law.
- Publisher: EUR-Lex.
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32015L2366
- Accessed: 2026-05-16.
- Used by facts:
  - `FACT-PSD2-STRONG-CUSTOMER-AUTHENTICATION`
- Relevant articles and quotes:
  - Article 4(30): definition of strong customer authentication as
    authentication "based on the use of two or more elements
    categorised as knowledge ... possession ... and inherence".
  - Article 97: payment service providers shall apply strong customer
    authentication when the payer accesses payment accounts online,
    initiates an electronic payment transaction, or carries out any
    action through a remote channel which may imply a risk of payment
    fraud or other abuses.
  - Article 95: payment service providers shall have a framework with
    mitigation measures and control mechanisms to manage operational
    and security risks; competent authorities receive evidence of those
    measures.
  - Article 96: payment service providers report major operational or
    security incidents to the competent authority.
- Notes: Use this source when BI reports cover SCA outcomes, exemption
  reasons, channel categories, or payment-incident reporting. The
  recordkeeping / supervisory-inspection framing is supported by the
  Article 95 / 96 / 97 cluster, not the bare Article 4 definition.
